Model

Two point masses attract with F = G m₁m₂ / r² along the line joining them. Internal forces only, so total momentum and angular momentum are conserved and the centre of mass drifts at constant velocity.

Reduction to one body

The relative vector r = r₂ − r₁ obeys r̈ = −μ r / r³ with μ = G(m₁+m₂). That is a single Kepler problem; each body traces a scaled copy of it about the COM.

Orbit type from energy

Specific energy ε = v²/2 − μ/r: ε < 0 ellipse, ε = 0 parabola, ε > 0 hyperbola. Eccentricity e = √(1 + 2εh²/μ²), period T = 2π√(a³/μ).

Integrators

Explicit Euler (1st order, not symplectic) gains energy; symplectic Euler and velocity Verlet keep the energy error bounded; RK4 is 4th order but slowly drifts. Race them at a large Δt to see the difference.
